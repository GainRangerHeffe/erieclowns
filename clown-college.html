Clown College

Eight weeks of clowning around

Learn, laugh and perform. Clown College meets one night a week for eight weeks each fall at Kury Hall. Ages 8 and up; kids join with a parent or guardian.

This year's class is already underway. Join the list and we'll let you know when the next class opens, including the early registration deadline.

What you'll learn

  • Clown makeup and character
  • Looking, walking and acting like a clown
  • Skit and routine building
  • Props and gags
  • Costuming
  • Audience interaction and improv

Classes end with a graduation celebration, and graduates can join the alley for events and parades.
