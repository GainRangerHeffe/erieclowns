Community and nonprofit events

Rates and guidelines

We get lots of requests from charities and organizations, and we'd love to say yes to everyone. As a volunteer-run Pennsylvania nonprofit, though, we can only take on so many events each year, and the cost of balloons and face paint keeps rising. These guidelines help us keep showing up.

Community and nonprofit eventsOne-time donation that covers travel, balloons and face paint
$100 min.
Private parties (referral)Booked directly with a member clown; rates and availability vary
~$150/hr
For comparisonTypical professional rate, per clown
