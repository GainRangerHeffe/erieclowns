About us

A clown alley with its own hall

Joe and Jill Kury started the Erie Clowns in 1968, and members finished building our 5,000 sq ft home, Kury Hall, in 1972. Today the ERIEsistible Clowns are a nonprofit of more than 50 members who perform, march in parades, visit hospitals, and still get together for picnics and Christmas parties.

Our mission is simple: make people from 1 to 100 smile and laugh.
